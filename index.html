01 / REVIEW

Start with your records

Review Garmin activity details, pace and heart-rate charts, and available sleep and HRV data using your own Personal API key.

02 / PLAN

Give your goals context

Set a race or fitness goal. Connect a compatible external AI assistant through local MCP to discuss your records and save training drafts.

03 / TRAIN

Keep the final say

Review and manage structured workout drafts. Publishing workouts to Garmin Connect is planned and is not available in this prototype.

What is available today?

The open-source prototype runs on your own computer. The website and MCP server share local records, goals and workout drafts. AI recommendations happen in the external assistant you connect; this public website does not analyze your health data.

A hosted multi-user service, Garmin OAuth and workout delivery, and mobile apps are future plans. There is no public account registration or paid subscription on this website.
